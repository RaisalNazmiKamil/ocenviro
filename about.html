Who We Are

Innovating Marine & Coastal Engineering Solutions

Established in 2010 in Bandung, PT. OC ENVIRO is a premier engineering consulting services company specializing in survey, site investigation, and ocean engineering design.

The primary motivation behind establishing OC ENVIRO is to deliver innovative and integrated solutions to address the technical and environmental challenges faced by stakeholders across the marine industry sector in Indonesia.

Our Vision

"To be a leading Indonesian ocean engineering consultant emphasizing innovation and professionalism to create integrated and sustainable engineering solutions for present and future stakeholders."
